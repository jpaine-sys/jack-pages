Products in Scope

Product Reference

What each product family does and why it matters for Wiley US.

Sales Cloud

The CRM platform managing Wiley's full sales cycle from lead to close. The Unlimited Edition gives sales and service teams shared access to pipeline management, AI forecasting, automation, and unrestricted customisation across all 2,000 users.

Service Cloud

Customer service and support platform enabling Wiley's service team to handle cases across every channel from a single workspace. Includes Omni-Channel routing, knowledge management, SLA tracking, and Einstein-powered case deflection.

Agentforce for Service

Autonomous AI agents that resolve customer enquiries end-to-end across messaging, email, and chat without requiring a human agent. Built on Data Cloud unified profiles and grounded in Wiley's knowledge base and policies.

Data Cloud

Real-time customer data platform that unifies profiles from Salesforce apps, third-party systems, and event streams. Powers personalisation, segmentation, and the compute layer for all Agentforce agents and Flex Credit workloads at Wiley.

Marketing Cloud Account Engagement

B2B marketing automation platform (formerly Pardot) for lead generation, nurturing, and scoring. The Premium tier includes multiple business units, custom objects, and advanced analytics, with tight Sales Cloud integration for lead handoff.

Marketing Cloud Engagement

Enterprise B2C email and cross-channel marketing platform for Wiley's content and subscription marketing. Handles personalised emails, automated journeys, and transactional messages across 7 business units and 40+ sending domains.

Experience Cloud (Communities)

Branded self-service portal connected to Salesforce data. Wiley's customer community supports 502 named members plus a login-based pool of up to 12,000 logins per month for lower-frequency users.

Own Archive

Data archiving and backup solution that moves infrequently accessed Salesforce records to long-term storage while keeping them searchable. Helps Wiley manage storage costs in production without losing access to historical data.
