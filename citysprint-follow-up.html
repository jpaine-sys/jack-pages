Our Proposal

Connect what you have.
Activate what you've built.

CitySprint already has Snowflake, Data Cloud credits, and Marketing Cloud. The investment is in connecting and activating not starting over.

The Data Layer

One customer view across CityTrack, CRM, and Snowflake

Right now those three systems are separate. Connecting them creates the foundation for every outcome below attribution, churn prevention, personalised campaigns.

🔗
Snowflake connects without moving data
Zero-copy integration means the Snowflake investment CitySprint has already made becomes usable for the first time. No rework needed.
⚠️
At-risk accounts identified before they leave
Scoring based on dropping booking frequency and service complaint history. Retention journey fires automatically not after the customer has already called to cancel.
📊
Marketing spend connected to won revenue
Closes the loop between a paid media click and an account in CRM. Fixes the 75% Direct problem and gives Jen the proof she needs to invest with confidence.
🎯
Segments built on real delivery behaviour
Not just CRM fields actual shipment frequency, sector, and service type. Campaigns go to the right accounts for the right reason.
💡
Already in the contract
Data Cloud credits and ~30k Agentforce conversation credits are included via the Foundations agreement. The cost here is activation not new licence spend.
Campaigns and Journeys

Campaigns that run on triggers, not manual effort

An in-place upgrade from the Marketing Cloud licence CitySprint is already on. No migration. The same platform significantly more capable.

Automation

Journeys that fire without manual work

DSU onboarding, lapsed customer win-back, and same-day to next-day upsell all triggered by behaviour from the data layer, not a manual list export.

Attribution

Prove ROI on every pound of media spend

Attribution across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn already active, needs connecting to close the loop to revenue. Directly answers Jen's requirement before she'll invest further.

Capacity

More campaigns without more headcount

AI-generated audience segments and campaign briefs mean a lean team can run significantly more without needing to grow the function.

"Marketing was previously semi-detached. Nothing off the table if it drives revenue."
Faye Calland
Sales Director, CitySprint
Revenue Growth

Three revenue opportunities that aren't being captured

Retention gets the headline but there's meaningful growth available from existing accounts too.

Upsell

Same-day customers aren't seeing next-day

No rules engine, no segmentation. A customer who books same-day every week has never been shown a next-day option. That's a revenue conversation that's never happening.

Booking platform has no promo logic or upsell rules
Abandoned quotes get no follow-up at all
Cross-sell

SME revenue down 34% with no early signal

SME revenue has declined 34% over four years. Without delivery behaviour data connected to CRM, there's no way to see which accounts are quietly pulling back before they leave.

Segmentation on real usage enables targeted account plays
Sales get next-best-action signals, not just a contact list
Win-back

Lapsed accounts have no reactivation journey

Booking inactivity currently goes unnoticed. A win-back journey triggered on lapsed behaviour even at 2–3% reactivation moves meaningful revenue given the account base size.

Lapsed customer count unknown this is a data point we need
Journey can be built once the data layer is connected
