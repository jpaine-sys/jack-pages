01
Unified Data Ingestion
Replacing manual uploads with automated, centralised data intake
90-100% downstream tracking
The problem today
Post-event vendor and pavilion scan data is processed manually via spreadsheets. Teams create duplicate records so separate internal teams can each claim lead ownership, meaning the same contact appears multiple times across different systems with no resolution.
The simple fix
Use Data360 strictly to ingest scan spreadsheets automatically. Rather than configuring complex deduplication logic upfront, centralise data intake at source, bypassing the manual tidying entirely and giving every downstream journey a clean starting point.
OKR alignment: OBJ01 Holistic strategy OBJ02 Automate processes
02
Baseline Send Visibility
A send log that acts as a coordination dashboard before full governance is in place
30% reduction in unplanned sends
The problem today
Contacts receive 7-8 uncoordinated emails a day from different GSMA teams. Full frequency cap automation would stall because capping rules, priority rankings, and governance ownership across BUs are not yet defined.
The simple fix
Use Data360 to ingest all BU send histories into a single centralised Send Log. Do not enforce blocking logic yet. Treat this as an operational "thermometer" so teams can visually coordinate send timing before executing, reducing clashes through awareness rather than automation.
OKR alignment: OBJ01 30% send reduction OBJ03 Optimised campaigns
03
Basic Digital Onboarding Journeys
Replacing manual welcome campaign blasts with a single automated trigger
25% conversion uplift on priority journeys
The problem today
65-75% of GSMA's current marketing effort is spent on manual email blasts rather than structured engagement. New members have no automated welcome or onboarding sequence, entry into any journey relies on manual list work.
The simple fix
Replace rigid campaign blasts with a single linear automated welcome track for new members. The journey fires uniformly upon a subscription status change in the source data, requiring zero ongoing manual administration once live.
OKR alignment: OBJ03 MQL conversion OBJ04 Personalised experiences OBJ05 Community growth
04
Commercial and Paid Media Sync
Stopping ad spend on leads already in a sales cycle
+20% marketing-influenced pipeline
The problem today
Commercial data sits siloed in Sales Cloud while paid advertising on Google and Meta runs independently, optimised for lead quantity rather than pipeline value. Leads already in an active sales cycle continue to receive generic acquisition ads.
The simple fix
Use Data360 to sync active opportunity stages from Sales Cloud directly with paid ad platforms. When a lead converts to an active sales cycle, Data360 automatically pushes them to a suppression list, instantly stopping wasteful acquisition ads without requiring custom scoring algorithms.
OKR alignment: OBJ03 Pipeline value OBJ04 Personalised experiences
05
Simple Propensity Score: AI Membership Churn Prediction
Flagging at-risk members before renewal using out-of-the-box AI
10% uplift in digital engagement
The problem today
There is a complete lack of reporting on member usage data. The account team cannot proactively flag drop-off risks or demonstrate ongoing membership value before standard annual renewal cycles. At-risk members go undetected until it is too late.
The simple fix
Use native AI Studio features to build an out-of-the-box propensity model on two existing data points: Member Gateway login recency and working group attendance records. The model automatically flags members with dropping activity as "At Risk" and drops them into re-engagement loops months before renewal, requiring no custom scoring build.
OKR alignment: OBJ03 MQL conversion OBJ05 Community growth OBJ06 Member engagement
06
Natural Language Segment Creation
Cutting a 3-week manual bottleneck down to minutes
Operational velocity and decentralised marketing
The problem today
A 15-person marketing operations team is consumed by manual data extension requests. Marketers do not use the platform directly and building standard audience segments manually takes up to three weeks. Strategic campaign decisions are blocked by a technical queue.
The simple fix
Once the unified data profile is live, marketers use natural language segment building inside Data Cloud to describe their target audience in plain text. Data extensions are generated instantly. The three-week bottleneck is removed and marketers can make and execute strategic decisions without raising a technical request.
